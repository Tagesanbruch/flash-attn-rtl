\section{Discussion}





\subsection{Necessity of a Vector Unit}

While \MSAGA{} is capable of executing \FA{} entirely on the
systolic array without relying on a vector unit, a vector unit or
SFU is still necessary in a full
accelerator system to support activation functions outside of \FA{}.
However, because \MSAGA{} offloads all attention-related computation
from the vector unit, the overall vector \FLOPs{} requirement may be
significantly reduced.





\subsection{Limitations of \MSAGA{}}

Although \MSAGA{} delivers high \FLOPs{} utilization for \FA{},
it is not well-suited for the decoding phase of LLM inference.

\MSAGA{} is ideal for the (1) LLM training and (2) prefill phase of LLM inference,
where the query matrix $Q$ has a long sequence length, making the
attention computation compute-intensive~\cite{splitwise}. In this case, the high
\FLOPs{} throughput of \MSAGA{} effectively accelerates the workload.

In contrast, during the decoding phase, the query $Q$ has a
sequence length of 1, making the computation memory-bound.
Moreover, since \MSAGA{} operates on $128 \times 128$ tiles,
$Q$ must be padded to fit this shape, resulting in substantial
wasted computation and inefficient resource usage.
