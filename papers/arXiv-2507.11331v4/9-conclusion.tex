\section{Conclusion}

We present \MSAGA{}, an enhanced systolic array architecture that enables
the entire \FA{} computation within a single systolic array.
Building on \MSAGA{}, we introduce \SA{}, an optimized kernel that efficiently overlaps all \FA{}
operations through careful scheduling and static dataflow management.
The experimental results validate our approach,
demonstrating significantly higher \FLOPs{} utilization than state-of-the-art accelerators,
with negligible accuracy loss and area overhead.
